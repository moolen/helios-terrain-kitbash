Mare Tranquillitatis pit DTM, whole footprint (NAC_DTM_TRANQPIT1)

samplemoon
Moon, Mare Tranquillitatis, strip 7.91-8.86N 33.17-33.25E; pit at 8.3355N 33.222E · DEM raster (LROC NAC stereo DTM, GeoTIFF float32) + orthoimages · 2 m/post DTM; orthos 0.6-0.7 m (M137332905, M152655237, M152662021, M155016845, M155023632) and 2 m · 4.74 x 28.78 km strip (N-S); 8.6 % no-data (pit interior, strip edges)
mare plaincollapse pit / skylightsmall fresh craters with bright ejectasubdued older craterslow swells

THE landmark source. The whole footprint shows what the 12 km map's core really looks like: a flat mare (172 m total relief over 29 km, mostly a gentle N-S tilt) peppered with 10-300 m craters, a few crisp fresh ones with bright blocky haloes, and the pit sitting on open, unremarkable mare. The strip is only 4.7 km wide, so it gives a real 4.7 x 6 km (or longer) core that must be padded E-W with Kaguya TC. Reused from io-data; not re-downloaded.

Mare Tranquillitatis pit close-up + the NAC pit image set (incl. oblique M144395745L)

samplemoon
Moon, 8.3355N 33.222E · DEM raster (LROC NAC stereo DTM, GeoTIFF float32) + orthoimages · DTM 2 m; NAC images 0.43-0.81 m/px · DTM 500 x 500 m; photos ~280 m zooms and ~800 m context
skylight pitoverhanging layered basalt wallfunnelboulder-strewn floor

Pit facts (LROC Pits Atlas #3): funnel 140-146 m wide and ~20 m deep, inner pit 88-100 m, floor 105 m below the funnel, vertical walls visible to ~80 m with a recess at ~40 m, overhangs >=10-15 m on E, W and N, a flat boulder-strewn floor, floor sloping down under the E wall. The DTM leaves the interior blank, so walls and floor have to be authored from the photos: M144395745L (50 deg oblique, the famous layered-wall view) and M175057326R (0.43 m, crisp rim and layers) for the wall bands; M155016845R (Sun 10.6 deg from zenith) for the floor boulders; M126710873R for the classic look. ODE lists 85 NAC images of the pit; 6 are used here.

Olympus Mons NW caldera rim, 12 km CTX frame at the current anchor

samplemars-volcanic
Mars, Olympus Mons summit, centre 18.4641N 226.1554E · CTX stereo DTM mosaic (USGS controlled, geoid-adjusted) + CTX orthoimage · 20 m DEM, 5 m ortho resample · 16 x 16 km mosaic, 12 x 12 km sample
caldera wall 1.4-2 kmspur-and-gully wallsummit lava plain with flow lobescollapse terrace with graben/ridges

Real 20 m terrain for the whole map, replacing the MOLA + authored wall: a scalloped wall with regular spur-and-gully buttresses (spacing ~800-1000 m), a sharp lip, the terrace floor in the E with arcuate graben and wrinkle ridges, and the summit plain in the W with low lobate flows and channels. Use CTX as the base and kit-bash the detail. Layout (hubs, ledge) in layout.png keeps the current game layout.

Jezero western delta, full 12 km map from the USGS Mars 2020 HiRISE DTM mosaic

samplemars-jezero-kit
Jezero crater, Mars; centre 18.47N 77.37E (map anchor), -6..6 km in an AEQD frame · HiRISE stereo DTM mosaic (GeoTIFF, Float32), range-read crop · 1 m posting (native); sample at 3 m (4000 px heightmap); 1 m crop kept in work/hirise_map_1m.tif · Mosaic 21.4 x 21.5 km (77.22-77.58E, 18.31-18.67N); covers the whole map. 1.9% gaps filled from the CTX 20 m DEM
inner rim massifsrim apronNeretva Vallis inletdelta top with channel belts and Belva craterlobate delta front scarpKodiak butteSeitah ridges and ripple fieldsMaaz cratered floor

THE base for the map: real 1 m terrain for every unit. Measured: rim crest -1920..-2080 m (local massifs to -1750), floor -2550..-2610 m, delta top -2400..-2490 m. The delta does NOT meet a cliff: the inner rim is a 1.5-2 km concave apron (median 11-12 deg, p99 29 deg at 1 m) and the delta apex merges into it at about -2380 m, i.e. 25-35 % of the rim relief above the floor. The real cliffs are the delta-front scarps (60-80 m) and Kodiak (75 m). See extra-profiles-ew.png. extra-floor-tint-proposal.jpg maps the real CTX albedo onto the measured Mars palette: the floor is mottled warm brown with dark ripple/sand bands and pale Maaz/Seitah outcrops.
