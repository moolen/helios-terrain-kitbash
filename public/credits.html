Authored parts

Lava spread, sulfur deposition masks, frost, scatter placement, material colour, supplemental relief and the pit shaft are authored or procedural. The source notes distinguish those from measured topography. Heightmap resolution is not necessarily measurement resolution.

Photo/reference cards preserve their original source credit. The selected archive omits unrelated raw scans, credential-dependent downloads and the Holuhraun UAS NC-SA sample.

Moon / Shackleton and Marius Hills

Measured regional bases: LRO LOLA 118 m global DEM, NASA/GSFC LOLA team; USGS Astrogeology, public domain. LROC pit measurements inform authored Marius landmark geometry. Terrain detail uses credited Poly Haven CC0 regolith and rock materials. Local channel walls, pit shapes, craters, terraces and placements are authored. The native game is represented by original render captures; the public viewers expose the preserved measured bases.
